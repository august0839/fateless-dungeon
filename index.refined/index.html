    Welcome to Fateless Dungeon

    TL;DR
    • Story-first: Narrative and creativity drive everything.
    • Floats replace dice: Infinite nuance instead of 1d20 limits.
    • AI interprets, never controls: The system translates math into story, while players and DMs stay in charge.

    What is Fateless Dungeon?

    Fateless Dungeon is a new way to experience tabletop RPGs.
    It rebuilds the foundation of role-playing into something modular, intuitive, and narrative-driven.

    Instead of drowning in rules or dice rolls, you focus on storytelling. The system quietly handles the numbers behind the scenes, turning raw math into emotional, story-ready outcomes.

    Why Fateless Dungeon?

    Traditional systems like Dungeons & Dragons are beautiful, but also intimidating. They can demand:
    - Rule mastery before play
    - Heavy time investment
    - A constant tug-of-war between mechanics and immersion

    Fateless Dungeon removes those barriers while keeping the heart of role-playing intact. You still have full control over your characters and worlds—AI just does the background interpretation so you don’t have to.

    How does it work?

    Fateless Dungeon replaces dice with floats—decimal values between 0.0 and 1.0.

    Where dice give you 20 outcomes, floats give you infinite nuance. A float like 0.47 isn’t just a “fail” or “success.” It’s a texture of possibility.

    What does the AI do?
    - It doesn’t roll for you.
    - It doesn’t play the game.
    - It doesn’t make decisions.

    It interprets floats into feeling. For example:

    Stealth Check: Your rogue rolls a 0.47.

    Instead of “fail,” the AI describes it: “You stumble over a loose stone, a sharp sound in the silence—but the guard coughs at that exact moment, covering the noise. Close call.”

    The float is math. The AI makes it narrative.

    What does this mean for your campaign?

    It means you can: - Build worlds through storytelling, not spreadsheets.
    - Create characters through backstory, not number slots.
    - Let AI turn floats into natural language so immersion never breaks.

    Example – Character Creation:
    Instead of typing stats, you tell a story: - “She was raised on a farm, hardened by chores.” → Strong Body.
    - “She spent nights memorizing folklore.” → Strong Mind.
    - “She clings to faith in her goddess.” → Strong Soul.

    From your words, floats are generated. You never see them, but the AI does—and interprets them into intuitive, expressive stats.
